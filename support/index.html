What Bukkit is

Bukkit is a budget for two people who run one home. You each add what you spend on your own iPhone, in the way that suits you, and you both see the same plan, the same spending and what is left this month.

Sharing with your partner

  1. Both of you need an iPhone signed in to iCloud.
  2. One of you opens Bukkit and starts the budget. On the page "Invite your partner", tap Send invite and send the link in Messages. You can also do this later: tap your initial at the top right of Home to open Settings, tap your name card at the top ("Your own budget · Invite someone"), then Send invite.
  3. Your partner opens the link on their iPhone, then opens Bukkit and types their name.
  4. From then on, Us items are shared between you. Anything you mark as Personal stays with you only and never reaches your partner's iPhone.

Bukkit works on its own too. Until someone joins, everything you add goes into the household, ready to share. Right before you send the invite, you can pick anything you would like to keep Personal.

To stop sharing, open Settings and tap your name card.

Deleting your data

  • One item: open a spend, bill, goal or asset and tap Delete (or swipe it left). A spend is deleted together with any money back you added to it; a goal with its saved amounts; an asset with its past values. One past value of an asset can be deleted on its own too, and deleting a group moves its categories to the group you pick.
  • A copy for yourself: Settings › Backup and export makes a spreadsheet file (CSV) of what you can see and hands it to the share sheet. Bukkit keeps no copy.
  • This iPhone: delete the Bukkit app. Everything kept only on this iPhone goes with it.
  • Everything in your iCloud: in your iPhone's Settings, open your iCloud storage, choose Bukkit and delete its data.
  • A shared household lives in the iCloud of the person who started it. Deleting an Us item removes it for both of you.

Two things the app no longer says on its screens

Why is a card payment or a move to savings not in my spending? Card payments and transfers to savings are moves of money, not spending, so Cash flow never counts them twice. The purchase itself is what counts.

Where do the prices of my stocks come from? Bukkit does not fetch prices. You type a price by hand for every Korean or US share, and the asset's value is the shares times the price you typed.

Exchange rates

Your budget is in one currency, picked from your iPhone's region when you start. You can change it in Settings > Currencies until you set your first plan or goal. Spends, money in and bills in another currency keep their own currency and count in your budget's at the European Central Bank's reference rate for that day, through Frankfurter; assets count at the latest rate. Rates between two currencies other than the euro are worked out from their euro rates. Only the currency codes and dates are sent.
